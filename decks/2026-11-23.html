GPT-6.2 Astra ships to harsh reviews — but tops the med-chemistry benchmark

  • OpenAI released GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol on Nov 17; early reception was sharply negative — one prominent tester called it the 'worst model OpenAI has ever released'
  • Yet on MedChemBench (a 99-item medicinal-chemistry task set) Astra earned 84.5% — a leading score, though the benchmark itself cautions it does not prove real drug-discovery capability
  • Google answered Nov 18 with Gemini 4 Neon at $3/$15 per million tokens and a one-million-token context window — the price war continues

Why it matters: Frontier labs now ship medical-science benchmarks alongside each release — a clear signal of where they think the value is heading.
